## 3. Adaptations & Modifications (CC BY-SA 4.0 §3(a))

The 3D assets in `public/muscle/models/*.glb` and `src/muscle/models/manifest.json` are adaptations of the Z-Anatomy Blender atlas:

1. **Curriculum Selection:** Extracted surface skeletal landmarks, superficial muscles, deep postural stabilizers, and major articular joints required for artist training, filtering out internal visceral organs.
2. **Topology Decimation:** Decimated triangle counts using Blender and `gltf-transform` to adhere to web performance budgets (≤ 25,000 tris per mesh, ≤ 80,000 tris per regional GLB).
3. **Split-Plane Écorché:** Bisected the anatomy along the sagittal plane (+X right side for peelable study muscles, −X left side for skeletal reference).
4. **Coordinate Alignment & GLB Packaging:** Re-centered structures for Three.js camera framing and packaged into Draco-free `.glb` assets with cache-busting revision metadata.
